# Scoring Rubric

Score each dimension 1–10. Explain the score briefly.

## 1. Company / business model

### 9–10
Clear customer pain, credible team, strong evidence of repeatable monetization and durable value.

### 7–8
Attractive model with meaningful validation, but some stage or evidence risk remains.

### 5–6
Plausible but important assumptions remain unproven.

### 1–4
Weak value loop, unclear monetization, major execution or credibility concern.

## 2. Product / market space

Consider:
- urgency of problem
- market growth
- differentiation
- productization
- customer switching / adoption friction

## 3. Candidate-role fit

### 9–10
Most critical responsibilities have direct evidence at comparable complexity.

### 7–8
Strong core fit; gaps are mainly product/domain learning.

### 5–6
Several transferable capabilities but at least one meaningful responsibility gap.

### 1–4
Core success responsibilities lack evidence or contradict the candidate's profile.

## 4. Career growth value

Consider:
- skill compounding
- exposure to senior customers / complex problems
- market relevance
- future role optionality
- quality of team / manager

## 5. Industry / work-content interest

Only use explicit candidate preferences where available.

If preferences are unknown, mark **not enough information** rather than inventing a score.

## 6. Role boundary / sustainability

Consider:
- workload
- portfolio size
- implementation burden
- travel
- support functions
- conflicting KPIs
- accountability vs authority

## Pursuit priority

### A — Prepare seriously
High strategic value and fit. Remaining unknowns are manageable.

### B — Worth exploring
Promising but 1–3 material unknowns must be verified.

### C — Practice / information value
Not a top choice, but may provide learning, market information, or interview practice.

### D — Low priority
Material mismatch, weak company thesis, or unattractive role reality.

## Confidence

### High
Strong primary-source evidence + clear JD + candidate evidence.

### Medium
Reasonable evidence but some important items rely on inference or missing data.

### Low
Ambiguous company identity, sparse public data, incomplete JD, or no candidate evidence.
