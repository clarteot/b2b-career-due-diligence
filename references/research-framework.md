# Research Framework

## 1. Start with a decision, not a company encyclopedia

Open with:
- pursue verdict
- strongest reason to care
- biggest risk
- actual role type
- candidate fit level
- evidence confidence

Then provide the research behind the judgment.

## 2. Company foundation

Research:
- founding date and stage
- founder-market fit
- leadership complementarity
- financing history
- strategic investors / industrial ties
- ownership concentration when material
- hiring / expansion signals

Question: **Why is this team structurally credible to solve this problem?**

## 3. Business model

Map:

**buyer → pain → offer → usage → measurable outcome → renewal / expansion**

Distinguish:
- software revenue
- usage-based revenue
- transaction take rate
- professional services
- implementation fees
- hybrid models

Watch for a product company whose growth still depends heavily on bespoke labor.

## 4. Diagnostic metrics

Choose metrics that can disprove the model.

Examples:

### SaaS
- activation
- time-to-value
- logo retention
- GRR / NRR
- expansion rate
- customer outcome

### AI Agent
- POC-to-production
- weekly / monthly production usage
- task volume
- human takeover rate where relevant
- scenario expansion
- validated ROI

### Marketplace
- buyer repeat rate
- supplier repeat rate
- liquidity / match rate
- GMV retention
- disintermediation

### Service-heavy enterprise business
- implementation cycle
- gross margin
- services utilization
- customer concentration
- renewal

## 5. Product workflow

Always explain:

**input → system action → output → user → changed business behavior**

For AI products, determine whether the system:
- advises
- automates
- executes multi-step work
- still depends on human service delivery

## 6. Competition

Map four layers:
1. direct
2. adjacent
3. incumbent
4. substitute / internal build / manual process

Then evaluate moat:
- data
- workflow embedding
- domain expertise
- integrations
- switching cost
- ecosystem
- network effects
- delivery capability
- technical differentiation

## 7. Job reality

Translate the JD into responsibility ownership, not keywords.

Key responsibility domains:
- discovery
- sales
- solution / POC
- implementation
- adoption
- renewal
- expansion
- project management
- executive relationship
- product feedback
- technical support

## 8. Fit

Use evidence, not similarity of titles.

For every requirement, classify:
- direct match
- transferable match
- adjacent evidence
- clear gap

## 9. Decision

Separate:
- company attractiveness
- role attractiveness
- candidate capability
- candidate preference

A strong company can still be a poor career choice for a specific candidate.
