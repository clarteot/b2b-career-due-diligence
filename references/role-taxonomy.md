# B2B Role Taxonomy

Use responsibility ownership to classify the role.

## Customer Success
Primary outcomes:
- onboarding / adoption
- customer value realization
- retention
- sometimes expansion

Warning signs:
- “CS” owns cold acquisition or heavy new-logo quota
- customer success is defined only as reactive support

## Strategic / Enterprise Customer Success
Adds:
- complex stakeholder management
- executive alignment
- business-case / value realization
- multi-product / multi-business-unit expansion

## Account Management
Primary outcomes:
- commercial relationship
- renewal
- expansion
- account plan

May have less implementation ownership than CS.

## Solution Consulting / Pre-sales
Primary outcomes:
- discovery
- demo
- solution mapping
- POC
- technical / business proposal
- support sales conversion

## Implementation / Delivery
Primary outcomes:
- project launch
- configuration
- integration
- delivery milestones
- acceptance
- risk management

## Enterprise Sales
Primary outcomes:
- pipeline
- new-logo acquisition
- negotiation
- close

Complex enterprise sales may overlap with discovery and solution shaping.

## Business Consulting / Digital Transformation
Primary outcomes:
- diagnose business problem
- redesign process / operating model
- produce solution roadmap
- often manage transformation stakeholders

## Business-facing AI / FDE
Primary outcomes vary, but often combine:
- use-case discovery
- workflow decomposition
- rapid prototyping / technical solution work
- deployment
- user adoption
- feedback to product / engineering

Clarify technical depth and customer ownership.

## Hybrid role
Label “hybrid” when multiple responsibility domains are material.

Hybrid is not automatically negative.

Flag risk when:
- 3+ major domains are owned
- success metrics span conflicting goals
- support functions are absent or unclear
- customer load is high despite deep-project responsibilities

## Role-boundary map

For every role, attempt to identify owner for:
- new logo
- pre-sales
- POC
- implementation
- adoption
- technical escalation
- renewal
- expansion
- executive relationship
- product feedback
