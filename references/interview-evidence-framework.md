# Interview Evidence Framework

## Evidence story structure

For each selected case, prepare:

1. **Context** — customer, product, stage, problem
2. **Diagnosis** — what was actually wrong or uncertain
3. **Action** — what the candidate personally did
4. **Coordination** — who needed to be aligned
5. **Decision** — tradeoffs or judgment made
6. **Outcome** — measurable or qualitative result
7. **Capability proven** — what this case demonstrates for the target role

## Case selection rules

Choose cases that prove different dimensions. Avoid three stories that all prove the same thing.

Recommended case portfolio for B2B roles:
- discovery / root-cause analysis
- complex solution / delivery
- account growth / commercial expansion
- cross-functional recovery or escalation
- executive stakeholder management

## Gap-answer structure

When the candidate lacks direct domain or product experience:

1. acknowledge the true gap
2. name the transferable capability
3. provide evidence
4. explain what is new and learnable
5. avoid claiming false equivalence

Example pattern:

> “I have not owned an AI-agent deployment directly. The transferable part is complex business discovery and bringing a product into an existing customer workflow. In X project, I… The new layer I would need to build is agent capability boundaries and AI-specific value validation.”

## Interviewer-specific evidence

### HR / recruiter
Emphasize:
- career logic
- role motivation
- stable narrative
- basic fit
- practical constraints

### Hiring manager
Emphasize:
- how you work
- cases
- role-specific judgment
- ownership boundaries
- measurable outcomes

### Executive
Emphasize:
- business understanding
- strategic judgment
- why the market / company thesis makes sense
- how you create leverage, not just complete tasks
